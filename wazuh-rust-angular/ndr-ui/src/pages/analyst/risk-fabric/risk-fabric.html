Dynamic Unified Risk Scoring Architecture

Spec v2.1
Dynamic Risk Score = clamp(1, 100, (TechSeverity_Base × W_asset × W_identity × M_time))
  • 1. Baseline Technical Severity (0-100): Normalized from Wazuh XML rule level (0 to 15) using Level × 6.67.
  • 2. Asset Criticality Weight (W_asset): Mission-Critical (2.4x) on payment and domain controllers down to 0.4x on development sandboxes.
  • 3. Identity Tier Weight (W_identity): Domain Administrators, root, and C-Suite executive accounts scale severity up to 2.0x.
  • 4. Temporal Window Multiplier (M_time): Scales risk up during month-end close or off-hours, and reduces priority during maintenance windows.
  • 5. Executive Impact Narrative: Instantly translates low-level technical events into board-ready operational risk impact statements.
